## Question 4

### Paraphrase the Sleep Is Your Superpower video in your own words in brief.

- Sleep is essential for both physical and mental health.
- Good sleep improves learning, memory, and concentration.
- Lack of sleep negatively affects your concentration,focus and overall health in general
- Sleep deprivation can increase the risk of serious health problems.


## Question 5

### What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?

- Maintain a fixed sleeping time every day.
- Aim for around 7–8 hours of sleep each night.
- Avoid caffeine, especially later in the day.
- Avoid using phone before going to bed.
- Make sleep a priority instead of avoiding it for work or entertainment.

## Question 6

### Paraphrase the video - Brain Changing Benefits of Exercise.

- Exercising daily improves mood, energy and focus
- Regular exercise helps the hippocampus grow and improves long-term memory.
- Exercise strengthens the prefrontal cortex, improving focus and attention.
- Exercise increases beneficial neurotransmitters such as dopamine, serotonin, and noradrenaline.
- Exercise can make the brain stronger and more resilient over the long term.

## Question 7

### What are some steps you can take to exercise more?

- Exercise for at least 30 minutes, every day.
- Start with simple activities like walking or jogging.
- Go for short walks during study for breaks.
- Don't try to push the body limits initially only.
- Choose activities that I enjoy so that I can stay consistent.
- Exercise with friends so that it feels more motivating.
