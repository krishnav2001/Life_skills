# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1

**Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.**

The video explains that **grit—passion and perseverance—is an important factor in achieving long-term success**, often more important than talent or intelligence. It emphasizes staying committed, working hard, and continuing despite failures and difficulties.

## Question 2

**Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.**

The video explains the difference between a **fixed mindset**, where people believe their abilities cannot change and it is something that they are born with, and a **growth mindset**, where people believe they can improve through effort and learning. It highlights that embracing challenges, mistakes, effort, and feedback helps us grow and achieve more.

## Question 3

**What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**

**Internal Locus of Control:** It means believing that our actions, efforts, and decisions have a major influence on the results we achieve.

**Key point:** Taking responsibility for our actions gives us greater control over our lives and helps us stay motivated and persistent when facing challenges.

- Be open to feedback
- Learn from others

## Question 5

**What are your ideas to take action and build Growth Mindset?**

My ideas to build a growth mindset are:

- Take on challenges instead of avoiding them.
- Learn from mistakes and failures.
- Focus on effort, improvement, and progress rather than just results.
- Ask for feedback and use it to improve.
- Try different strategies when something does not work.
- Set small, achievable goals and gradually take on bigger challenges.
- Reflect regularly on what I learned and what I can do better next time.
