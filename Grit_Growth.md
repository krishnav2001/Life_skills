In the video, it is explained how a fixed mindset is when people believe that their abilities are something that they are born with and cannot change and how a growth mindset is when people believe that they can improve through effort and learning. It shows that taking on challenges, making mistakes, working hard and receiving feedback helps us to grow and achieve more.

## Question 3

**What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**

Internal Locus of Control: It is the conviction that the outcomes we attain are significantly influenced by our actions, efforts, and decisions. 

Key point: By accepting accountability for our actions, we are able to maintain a sense of control over our lives and remain motivated and persistent in the face of adversity.

## Question 5

**What are your ideas to take action and build Growth Mindset?**

My ideas to build a growth mindset are:

- Take on challenges instead of avoiding them.
- Learn from mistakes and failures.
- Focus on effort, improvement, and progress rather than just results.
- Ask for feedback and use it to improve.
- Try different strategies when something does not work.
- Set small, achievable goals and gradually take on bigger challenges.
- Reflect regularly on what I learned and what I can do better next time.
