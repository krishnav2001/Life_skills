# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (PoSH) - Video Resource Summary



---

## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Based on the provided workplace harassment and PoSH training videos, sexual harassment includes any unwelcome behavior of a sexual nature that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive environment. Key categories include:

### Physical Behaviour
* Unwelcome physical contact, touching, pinching, or invading personal space.
* Unwanted physical proximity, cornering, or blocking someone's movement.

### Verbal Behaviour
* Unwelcome comments, jokes, or stories with sexual overtones.
* Making sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, or asking intrusive personal questions.


### Non-Verbal & Digital Behaviour
* Displaying or sharing sexually suggestive images, posters, videos, GIFs, or messages.
* Sexually suggestive gestures, staring, or leering.
* Sending inappropriate messages or violating professional boundaries on chat platforms and email.

### Workplace Environment
* Creating a hostile work environment through pervasive inappropriate behavior, off-color jokes, or microaggressions.
* Implicit or explicit threats regarding work conditions based on an individual's reaction to unwelcome behavior.

## 2. What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

### If You Experience Harassment Directly

<Sequence>
  <Step title="Say No Directly" subtitle="Immediate Action">
    If safe to do so, clearly state to the individual that their behavior is unwelcome, offensive, and must stop immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Document Everything" subtitle="Record Keeping">
    Record dates, times, locations, details of the incident, names of potential witnesses, and preserve electronic evidence (emails, chat logs, screenshots).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report to the Internal Committee (IC) / HR" subtitle="Formal Escalation">
    File a formal written complaint with the HR department or Internal Committee (IC) in accordance with organizational policy.
  </Step>
</Sequence>

### If You Witness Harassment

1. **Offer Support:** Check in with the individual targeted and let them know you witnessed the behavior.
2. **Be an Active Bystander:** Intervene if appropriate by redirecting the situation or speaking up against unacceptable behavior.
3. **Report/Testify:** Share your factual account of the incident with HR or the Internal Committee during an investigation.
4. **Maintain Confidentiality:** Keep all details private to protect the dignity and safety of all parties involved.
