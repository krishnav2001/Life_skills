# Focus Management

## Question 1

### What is Deep Work?

Deep Work is the ability to focus without distraction on a demanding task. It means giving your complete attention to important work so that you can produce high-quality results and improve your skills.

---

## Question 2

### According to the author, how do you do deep work properly, in a few points?

1. Choose an important task 
2. Remove distractions 
3. Work with full concentration 
4. Set a specific time and place 
5. Take breaks 


---

## Question 3

### How can you implement the principles in your day-to-day life?

I can implement deep work in my daily life in the following ways:

- Keep my phone away.
- Turn off unnecessary notifications.
- Focus on one task at a time.
- Avoid social media and other distractions.
- Take short breaks after focused periods.

## Question 4

### What are the dangers of social media, in brief?

According to the author, social media can be dangerous because:

- It is highly distracting
- It can become addictive
- It wastes valuable time
- It can negatively affect mental health
- It interrupts deep work
